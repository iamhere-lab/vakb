Built with Heritage, Designed for Life

In the heart of Banashankari, South Bangalore, Vaishnavi Krishna Brindavan rises as a luxury high-rise address where modern living meets abundant greenery. A central courtyard, wrapped in lush landscaping and vibrant blooms, sits between two elegant towers — creating a calm, timeless oasis at the very core of the community.

The residences are planned to draw in natural light, openness and scenic views, with spacious 3 & 4 BHK layouts crafted for families who value expanse and refinement. Over an acre of forested garden, a signature clubhouse and an iconic infinity-edge pool complete an everyday experience that goes far beyond the ordinary.
