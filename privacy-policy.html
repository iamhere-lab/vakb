1. Who we are

This Website is an enquiry and information portal for the Vaishnavi AT-ONE Krishna Brindavan residential project (RERA No: PRM/KA/RERA/1251/310/PR/081025/008140). It is managed by an authorised marketing partner and is not the official website of the developer. The marketing partner that operates this Website is the entity responsible for the personal data collected through it.

We process personal data in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as applicable in India.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you fill in the enquiry form on the Website (the form in the page header, or the pop-up "Enquire Now" form), we collect:

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Preferred configuration (for example, 3 BHK or 4 BHK)
  • Your consent choice for being contacted

If you contact us by phone or WhatsApp using the numbers shown on the Website, we will also receive the details you choose to share in that conversation, along with your phone number.

Information collected automatically

When you submit an enquiry form, the following is attached to your submission automatically:

  • IP address of the device you are using (looked up through a public IP-lookup service when the page loads)
  • Page URL from which the form was submitted, including any campaign parameters in the link you arrived from
  • Date and time of submission (Indian Standard Time)
  • Form source (which form on the page you used)

Separately, our analytics and advertising tags (see Section 6) collect usage data such as the pages you view, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and browser type, and referring website or advertisement.

We do not ask for, and request that you do not submit, sensitive personal data such as financial account details, passwords, government ID numbers, or health information through this Website.

3. How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and share pricing, floor plans, brochures and site-visit details for the project
  • Contact you by phone call, SMS, email or WhatsApp to follow up on your interest
  • Forward your enquiry to the project's developer and its authorised sales team so they can assist you
  • Record the source of your enquiry (for example, which advertisement or campaign brought you to the Website) and measure the effectiveness of our marketing
  • Detect and prevent spam, duplicate or fraudulent submissions
  • Comply with applicable law and respond to lawful requests from authorities

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Consent & marketing communications

The enquiry forms include a consent statement authorising us and our partners to contact you via call, SMS, email and WhatsApp in connection with your enquiry, even if your number is registered on the National Do Not Call (DNC / NDNC) registry. By submitting the form with that consent, you give us this authorisation.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by telling the person who contacts you that you no longer wish to be contacted, by replying "STOP" to any SMS or WhatsApp message we send, or by reaching us using the details in Section 13. Once you withdraw consent we will stop contacting you about this project, except where we need to respond to a request you have made.

6. Cookies, analytics & advertising

The Website uses the following technologies:

  • Google Tag Manager – loads our measurement and advertising tags.
  • Google Ads conversion tracking (gtag.js) – records when a visitor who arrived from one of our Google advertisements completes an enquiry, so that we can measure and improve our advertising. Google may set cookies on your device for this purpose and may combine this data with other information it holds about you under its own Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps embed – the location map on the home page is served by Google and is subject to Google's privacy terms.
  • Google Fonts – typefaces are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address as part of the request.
  • Session storage – we store a small flag in your browser's session storage so that the enquiry pop-up is shown only once per visit. This is deleted when you close the browser tab and contains no personal data.

You can control cookies through your browser settings and can opt out of personalised advertising from Google at adssettings.google.com. Blocking cookies does not prevent you from using the enquiry form.

7. Where your data is stored & retention

Enquiry data is stored in Google Workspace services (Google Sheets) under an account controlled by us. Google may process this data on servers located outside India. By submitting an enquiry you consent to this transfer, which is carried out under Google's standard data-protection commitments.

We retain enquiry records for as long as is necessary to respond to your enquiry and for our legitimate business, accounting and legal purposes, after which the records are deleted or anonymised. Where you withdraw consent, we will stop active use of your data but may keep a minimal record to honour your request not to be contacted.

8. How we protect your data

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Transmitting form submissions over encrypted (HTTPS) connections
  • Restricting access to the enquiry log to authorised team members only
  • Using access-controlled, password-protected Google Workspace accounts

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately using the details in Section 13.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your data where it is no longer required for the purpose it was collected
  • Withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time
  • Raise a grievance about how your data has been handled

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the timelines required by applicable law.

10. Children's privacy

The Website is intended for adults interested in purchasing a residential property. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 years of age. If you believe a minor has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Third-party links

The Website may contain links to third-party websites, including the developer's official website and WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was most recently revised. Continued use of the Website after a change means you accept the updated Policy.
